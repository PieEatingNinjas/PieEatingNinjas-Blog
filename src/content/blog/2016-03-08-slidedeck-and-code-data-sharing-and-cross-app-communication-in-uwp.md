8th March 2016 I gave a talk for [Mobel](http://www.meetup.com/mo-bel/ "Mobel") ("Belgian Mobile Developer User Group") about Data sharing and Cross-App Communication in UWP. As it was only a short talk (in total there were 3 speakers, each one covering 'data sharing' on an other platform), I had to limit the content of my session. The things I covered were: Share Contracts, Protocol and App Services.
